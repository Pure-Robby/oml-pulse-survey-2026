Welcome to the Mini Pulse Culture Survey.

Your participation will help us to understand how you're feeling and how we can support each other during this time of change.

In answering this survey focus on the immediate team that you belong to. This is your proximal or nearest work group, the people you work with day-to-day and with whom you interact with on a regular basis in order to perform your work. You and your nearest team members are more likely to report to the same line manager/team leader.

Line managers should focus your responses on the team that you belong to and not on the team you manage.

About This Survey

  • Duration: Approximately 10 minutes to complete
  • Questions: 12 questions covering (1) Employee Engagement and (2) Change Resilience
  • Format: Rate your agreement with each statement using the scale provided
  • Optional comments: Share additional thoughts on each topic

What We'll Explore

lock Your Privacy is Protected

Your responses are confidential. The data of the survey will be used to create feedback reports on a company, business unit and team level. Data in these reports will be reported to managers and Old Mutual in aggregate groups of 5 or more respondents. Only the People Data Analytics team at Old Mutual will have access to your survey responses connected to your employee profile. In addition, all submitted comments are shared. Take care not to identify yourself in the comments unless you are comfortable to do so. For more information view the Pure Survey Data Protection Summary.

Independent Survey Management: This survey is being managed independently by Pure Survey, a professional survey platform specialising in confidential employee feedback. This ensures complete independence and neutrality in data collection and analysis.

How We Protect Your Privacy:

  • All responses are collected anonymously through Pure Survey's secure platform
  • Data is aggregated and analyzed at group level only
  • No personal identifiers are linked to your responses
  • Results are presented in summary format that cannot identify individuals
  • Independent management ensures unbiased data handling

Please answer honestly and openly. Your candid feedback is crucial for improving our workplace culture.
